02  Diagnosis

What is broken right now

Six problems run through the account. The first five are the reasons the spend was wasted. The sixth is the single worst settings choice in Google Ads, and it sat at the heart of the Search campaign.

1. No negative keyword work

The account has 4 negative keywords across 4 Performance Max campaigns, an average of one per campaign. A properly built account starts at 1,000 to 2,000 negatives and grows toward the limit of 10,000. Without them, Google pushed the campaigns broad and you paid for searches like "how much are mobile homes worth", "comparative market analysis cma", "arv calculator free", and even the single word "cash". None of those people are looking for your service.

2. No location exclusions

Performance Max does not just target the area you pick. You also have to actively exclude everywhere else, or the campaign shows internationally. You targeted Los Angeles but excluded nothing, so you almost certainly paid for clicks well outside your market. This is one of the main ways spam leads get in.

3. No spam defenses

Zero IP address exclusions. "Content suitable for families" is not excluded, which means kids' YouTube content was a valid placement, and a child is never going to request a home valuation. Apps are not excluded, and apps are both a haven for spam and a poor place to win conversions. There are no placement exclusions at all, where a correct setup would carry 50,000 or more. This combination invites the lead and conversion fraud that plagues Performance Max.

4. Weak ads and assets

Recent campaigns have headlines that do not even contain the word "home", so ads could show without making the offer clear, which pulls in the wrong clicks. There are only three descriptions where there should be five, and there are no videos. This pattern repeats across most of the Performance Max campaigns.

5. No ad extensions

No sitelinks, no structured snippets, and no callout extensions on any campaign. Extensions are free real estate on the results page. They raise click-through rate and give Google more reasons to show your ad higher. Leaving them off across the board is leaving performance on the table.

6. The Search campaign used the worst possible setting

The Search campaign was set to "Search with Display Select" included. This runs your text ads across the Display network, where they perform poorly, while flooding the campaign with low quality Display impressions that drag your click-through rate down. Because Quality Score is driven largely by click-through rate, this raises what you pay per click on Search. It is the worst of both worlds with none of the benefits. On top of that, the structure was just one ad group for phrase match and one for broad match, with only 39 negative keywords.

Why a restart will not work: Turning these campaigns back on would re-open the same wide funnel with the same missing fences. The spend would resume against the same irrelevant searches and the same spam placements. The structure itself is the problem, so the structure is what we rebuild.

05  Destinations

Where the traffic should land

The destination matters as much as the targeting. The ads currently point to standalone pages at cashofferal.com and socalhomevalue.com, and they send your paid traffic away from your main website, the asset that needs it most given the SEO issue.

What is on the pages now

Both current ad destinations were reviewed live on 2026-05-28. Each is a single, thin, templated page: a headline, a subheadline, and a seven field form. Neither gives a visitor a reason to trust before asking for their address, name, email, and phone.

cashofferal.com

Headline: "Get an Above Market Cash Offer"
Subheadline: "Avoid commissions and costly repairs. Get a direct home offer and choose your closing date"

  • One call to action, a "NEXT" button on a seven field form (address, city, state, ZIP, name, email, phone) plus an SMS consent box.
  • No trust signals: no reviews, testimonials, company information, or homes-bought numbers.
  • No content beyond the form. Thin and templated.

socalhomevalue.com

Headline: "SEE YOUR HOME VALUE INCREASE"
Subheadline: "See The Most Accurate Home Value With A Custom Report"

  • One call to action, a "NEXT" button on the same seven field form, asking for full contact details up front.
  • No trust signals: no reviews, no agent credentials, no explanation of how the valuation is produced.
  • The headline promises a value increase rather than answering the valuation search intent. Thin and templated.
The pattern on both pages: each one demands seven fields, including a phone number, before giving anything in return, and offers nothing to earn that trust first. That combination of a high ask and zero credibility is a conversion killer, and it confirms there has been no real optimization work on these links.

06  Build strategy

How we build the account properly

The rebuild is built on one principle: relevance first. Every decision below exists to put your ad in front of someone searching for exactly what you offer, in your area, and to keep everyone else out. Here is the blueprint, in the order we build it.

5.1  Separate the two services

Cash Offer and SoCal Home Value are different businesses with different buyers. A homeowner wanting a fast cash sale is in a very different mindset from someone curious what their home is worth. Mixing them, as the current account does, blurs the targeting and the messaging for both. We split them into their own campaigns, each pointed at its own landing page, with its own keywords, negatives, and ads. This alone removes a large share of the irrelevant traffic, because each campaign now speaks to one clear intent.

Cash Offer (cashofferal.com)

Intent: sell my house fast, sell as is, cash buyer, avoid agent fees. Audience is motivated sellers. Ads lead with speed, certainty, and no fees or repairs.

SoCal Home Value (socalhomevalue.com)

Intent: what is my home worth, free home valuation, home value estimate. Audience is exploring, often earlier in the journey. Ads lead with a fast, accurate, local valuation.

5.2  Campaign architecture

Search is the backbone, because Search captures people typing exactly what they want at the moment they want it. We build each service as a pure Search campaign in SKAG format. Performance Max comes back only after Search proves the funnel converts, and only when built correctly with the full set of exclusions. We do not lead with Performance Max into an unproven funnel, and we never again run Search with Display attached.

5.3  SKAG structure and why it wins

SKAG means single keyword ad groups: one keyword per ad group across its match types, with that keyword written into the ad. If we target 40 keywords, we build 40 ad groups. It is more work to set up, and it is the reason the ad on the right beats a generic ad. When the search and the ad match closely, Google bolds the matching words, the ad stands out, click-through rate rises, and Quality Score rises with it. Higher Quality Score means a lower cost per click and a higher position for the same budget.

Current generic ad

Adsocalhomevalue.com
Sell Your Property Fast | Get Started Today
Find out more about our services and offers. Contact us today to learn how we can help with your property.

Search term: "online house valuation". The ad never says home or valuation, so the click could come from anyone.

SKAG ad for the same search

Adsocalhomevalue.com/home-value
Online House Valuation | Free Local Home Value Report
See what your SoCal home is worth in minutes. Accurate, local, no obligation. Get your free valuation now.

Keyword in the headline and URL. Google bolds the match, the ad is unmistakably relevant, and the click is far more likely to convert.

5.4  Negative keyword foundation

Before a single ad goes live, each service gets a researched negative list of 1,000 to 2,000 terms. This blocks the entire space that wasted your budget: free calculators, ARV and equity questions, tax assessment lookups, mobile home values, agent and legal questions, and pure research queries. From launch we review the search term report daily and add every new irrelevant term, growing the list toward the 10,000 limit over the first months. This is the single highest leverage activity in the account, and it is the one the current setup skipped entirely.

5.5  Geography and spam defenses

Los Angeles and the chosen SoCal radius are set as the only included locations, with an active exclusion list so nothing serves outside the area. On the spam side we exclude known bad IP addresses, exclude apps, exclude content suitable for families, and load a placement exclusion list of 50,000 or more. These steps shut the doors that let fraud and irrelevant clicks in. They are not optional extras, they are what separates a real account from a budget leak.

5.6  Ads, assets, and extensions

Every ad makes the offer obvious. Headlines include the service words a real buyer would type. Each ad carries five descriptions and the full asset set, including video for Performance Max. Every campaign gets sitelinks, callout extensions, and structured snippets. This raises click-through rate, improves Quality Score, and gives your ad more space and more reasons to be shown above competitors.

5.7  Measure qualified leads, not raw conversions

We confirm conversion tracking is firing correctly on both sites, then judge the account on qualified leads rather than raw form fills. This matters because fraud works by faking conversions. If we optimize toward raw conversions, we train Google toward the fraud. By optimizing toward leads that turn out to be real, motivated homeowners, we steer the budget toward the traffic that actually grows your business.

The result of the rebuild: a tightly targeted account where each service speaks to its own audience, the wrong traffic is fenced out before it costs you anything, every ad is clearly relevant, and the budget concentrates on people in your area who are searching to sell or value a home right now.
07  Rollout

Phased rollout plan

We build on foundations first, prove the model on Search, then scale. Nothing scales before it is proven, which protects your budget at every step.

Phase 1 · Days 1 to 14

Foundations and Search launch

  • Negative keyword research, 1,000 to 2,000 per service
  • Location targeting plus exclusion list
  • IP, app, family content, and placement exclusions
  • Conversion tracking verified on both sites
  • SKAG Search campaigns built and launched, one per service
  • Sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets added

Phase 2 · Days 15 to 45

Optimize and prove

  • Daily search term review, negatives added continuously
  • Tighten bids, pause weak keywords, expand winners
  • Test ad copy variations per ad group
  • Confirm cost per qualified lead is trending down
  • Lock the converting funnel before scaling

Phase 3 · Days 46 to 90

Scale

  • Launch fully fenced Performance Max per service
  • Expand budget on proven, profitable campaigns
  • Grow negative and placement lists toward limits
  • Add new keyword themes as data supports them
  • Monthly performance review and forward plan
